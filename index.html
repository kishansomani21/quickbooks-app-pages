Privacy Policy

929 Accountants — QuickBooks Online Invoice Integration
Last updated: 1 June 2026

This privacy policy explains how 929 Accountants ("we", "us", "our") handles information when you connect your QuickBooks Online company to our internal invoicing integration ("the Application"). The Application is used by 929 Accountants to create and manage sales invoices in QuickBooks Online on behalf of our clients.

1. Who we are

929 Accountants is an accountancy practice based in the United Kingdom. We act as the data controller for information processed through the Application. If you have any questions, contact us using the details at the bottom of this page.

2. What information we access

When a QuickBooks Online company is connected, the Application may access, through Intuit's official API:

  • Company information (company name, realm/company ID);
  • Customer records (names, addresses, email addresses) — read only, to match invoices to existing customers;
  • Product/service items and tax (VAT) codes — read only, to apply the correct line items and tax;
  • Sales invoices that the Application creates or updates.

The Application uses strict lookup: it does not create new customers or items in your books. It only reads existing records and writes sales invoices.

3. How we use the information

  • To create and (where requested) send sales invoices in your QuickBooks Online company;
  • To match invoice lines to the correct existing customers, items and VAT codes;
  • To maintain a secure connection to Intuit (storing the OAuth tokens needed to keep the link active).

We do not sell, rent, or share your QuickBooks data with any third party. We do not use it for advertising or profiling.

4. How information is stored

Authentication tokens and configuration are stored locally and securely on systems operated by 929 Accountants, with restricted file permissions. Access tokens are short-lived and refresh tokens are rotated regularly in line with Intuit's requirements. We retain data only for as long as the integration is in use.

5. Data sharing

The only third party involved is Intuit Inc. (the provider of QuickBooks Online), whose API the Application calls. Your use of QuickBooks Online is also governed by Intuit's own privacy statement.

6. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of personal data we hold, and to withdraw consent at any time. You can disconnect the Application at any time from within your QuickBooks Online account (Apps → Connected apps → Disconnect), which revokes our access.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above reflects the latest version.

8. Contact

For any privacy questions or requests, contact 929 Accountants by email at info@929accountants.co.uk.
